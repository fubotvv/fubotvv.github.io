Fubo TV Login Account

A Fubo TV login account allows subscribers to access their streaming service, manage account settings, review subscription information, and use supported devices. If you are having trouble signing in, checking your login details and following the appropriate account recovery steps can help resolve common access problems.

Before signing in, make sure you have the email address and password associated with your account. You should also use a secure and trusted internet connection when entering account credentials.

How to Log In to Your Fubo TV Account

Open the Login Page

Open the appropriate Fubo TV sign-in page or login portal from a trusted source using your preferred web browser.

Enter Your Email Address

Enter the email address associated with your Fubo TV account in the required login field.

Enter Your Password

Type your account password carefully. Check that Caps Lock is not enabled if your password is case-sensitive.

Select Sign In

Review the information you entered and select the sign-in option to access your account.

Complete Any Security Verification

If additional verification is requested, follow the instructions shown on the screen before continuing to your account.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log in to my Fubo TV account?

Open the appropriate Fubo TV sign-in page, enter the email address associated with your account, enter your password, and follow any additional verification instructions displayed.

What should I do if I forgot my password?

Use the password recovery option available on the appropriate account login page and follow the instructions to reset your password.

Why can't I sign in to my account?

Check that your email address and password are correct. You can also check your internet connection, update your browser, or try signing in from another supported device.

Can I access my account from different devices?

Account access may be available across supported devices. Follow the sign-in instructions displayed on the device you are using.

How can I keep my account secure?

Use a strong password, avoid sharing your credentials, keep your devices updated, and only enter account information on trusted websites.
